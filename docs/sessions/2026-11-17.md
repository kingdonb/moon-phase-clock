### Lessons, already applied (#20)

- Answering "No" at the device check threw away a good signed build. The script now records the signed commit in `dist/x.y.z/COMMIT` and reuses the build on re-run, so the passphrase is entered once per release.
- `monkey` did not bring the app to the foreground on the Pixel. The device check now uses `am start`, then sends the phone Home and reports the active launcher alias and the crash log, which are the checks done by hand tonight.
- `ask` crashed instead of answering "no" when there was no terminal.
